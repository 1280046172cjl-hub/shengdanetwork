03Information Collection and Use

When you install and play our mobile games (including "Tribe Defense: Monster Era" and our other published titles), our Apps collect and process the following necessary categories of information to ensure core gameplay functionality:

  • Device & Identifier Information — device model, OS version, system language, general region (country/region level derived from IP address, not precise GPS location), and device identifiers used for analytics, anti-fraud, or ad attribution (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID, which you may reset or disable for personalized ads at any time in your device settings).
  • Gameplay & Usage Data — your game level progress, character and item status, local save data, and general in-game interaction events.
  • Crash Reports & Diagnostics — crash stack traces and performance diagnostic data generated upon unexpected app errors or freezes, used solely to identify and fix bugs.
  • In-App Purchase Records (if applicable) — if the game offers In-App Purchases (IAP), all payments are processed securely via Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card number or bank account credentials; we only receive non-sensitive order tokens returned by Google Play in order to deliver in-game virtual items.
  • Technical & Network Permissions — the Apps only request basic permissions necessary for operation (such as INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), and never access sensitive permissions such as contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location without your explicit consent.

We use the above information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and operate core gameplay and save progress
  • To optimize game performance, fix bugs, and ensure stability
  • To provide customer support and process user requests

We do not collect information for the purpose of selling it, and we do not collect personal information that is not necessary for the purposes described above.

04Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell users' personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may only be disclosed under the following limited and compliant circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers / SDKs — to support core features, crash analytics, or monetization, some of our games may integrate Google-certified third-party SDKs (for example: Google Play Services, Google Play Games Services, Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics, or Google AdMob). These providers process limited technical data strictly in accordance with their own privacy policies and the Google Play data safety requirements.
  • Legal Compliance — when required by applicable laws, regulations, or valid legal processes.
  • Safety & Protection — when necessary to prevent fraud or cheating, or to protect the legal rights and safety of our users and the public.

Where such sharing occurs, we require the parties involved to handle information in accordance with this policy and applicable data protection laws.

05Children's Privacy

Our games and services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it completely as soon as possible.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at shengdanetworktechnology@gmail.com. We strictly comply with applicable children's privacy laws and the Google Play Families Policy.

06Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include access controls, HTTPS/TLS encryption of data in transit between our apps and servers, and regular review of our development and operational practices.

Please note that no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
