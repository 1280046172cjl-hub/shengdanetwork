03Information Collection

Our applications are currently under development. When our applications are released, they may collect the following categories of information:

  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, system language, and general region.
  • Usage information — such as the application features you use and general interaction events.
  • Crash reports — diagnostic data generated when an application stops unexpectedly, used to identify and fix problems.
  • Technical information — such as application version, performance metrics, and network status.

We collect this information only for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate our services
  • To improve application performance and stability
  • To improve the user experience

We do not collect information for the purpose of selling it, and we do not collect personal information that is not necessary for the purposes described above.

04Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may only be disclosed in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers that process data on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • When necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

Where such sharing occurs, we require the parties involved to handle information in accordance with this policy and applicable data protection laws.

05Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it as soon as possible. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

Where our applications are designed for a general audience, we comply with applicable children's privacy requirements, including the relevant Google Play Families policies.

06Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Please note that no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
