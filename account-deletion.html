Account and Data Requests

This page explains how to request account deletion and how to submit a personal data removal request.

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Current Status

shengdanetworktechnology is currently at an early development stage and has not released any game products. There is therefore no active account system at this time.

If our applications offer an account system in the future, this page will be updated accordingly, and users will be able to request account deletion or data removal by email.

01Requests You Can Submit

  • Account deletion — if you create an account in one of our applications in the future, you may request that the account be closed and deleted.
  • Data removal requests — you may request the deletion of personal data we hold about you, or ask us to explain what information we hold.

02How to Submit a Request

Please send an email to the address below, ideally from the email address associated with your account (if any), and include the following information:

  • Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" or "Data Removal Request";
  • State the name of the relevant application and your account identifier (if any);
  • Briefly describe your request so that we can verify and process it.

To protect your account security, we may ask you to provide additional information in order to verify your identity.

03How We Process Requests

Once we receive your request and complete identity verification, we will process it within a reasonable period, normally no longer than 30 days.

The relevant data will be deleted or anonymized. We will only retain specific information for as long as required by law or regulation, for example where retention is necessary for legal, tax, or security compliance.
